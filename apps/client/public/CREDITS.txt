Changes we made to the art (modified works):

  - Cropped: only the 16x16 cells and animation frames the game uses.
  - Repacked: frames rearranged into new sheets (sprites, the world tileset,
    the item icon sheet), with tile edges extruded by 1 px.
  - Combined: several pack tiles stacked into one tile (shores over water,
    statues over floors and similar), and the world map built from them.
  - Mirrored and rotated: room walls, corners and projectiles; the UI frames
    are rebuilt from the corners of Menu/Menu.png, mirrored into 9-slice frames.
  - Recoloured with colours of the pack's own palette: some floors, props and
    projectiles, hero palettes, the strokes of the UI frames (one colour per
    item rarity), and the gold sparkle used as a grey loot glint.
  - Outlined: a 1 px dark outline added to a few projectiles (bone, rock, spore).
  - Interiors of the UI frames filled with black or dark colours.
  The work was generated by tools/art and tools/world-gen of the game's source
  code; content/CREDITS.md in the source lists every source file and change.

Derived files shipped with the game (assets/ folder):

  - the sprite sheets and their .json frame data (from content/sprites);
  - the UI frames frame-*.png (from content/ui);
  - the world tileset world.png and the map overworld.tmj, which embeds it
    (from content/tilesets and content/maps).
